Built around the reveal moment

Ghost Draw is not a serious sketchbook. It is a compact creative toy for parties, daily prompts, awkward portraits, and drawings that become funnier once the hidden strokes appear.

Blind canvas

Your finger and brush cursor move, but the ink stays hidden until the reveal.

Ghost replay

The app plays every stroke back so the result feels like the ghost is drawing it for you.

Shareable results

Save the final card or export a local replay page that loops the drawing process.

Daily prompts, tools, gallery, and replay pages

Pick a prompt, choose a brush or shape tool, reveal the hidden picture, then keep the best weird results in your gallery. The replay page export is designed for the next step: hosted share links and a lightweight featured gallery.
