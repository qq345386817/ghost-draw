Ghost Draw

How to use Ghost Draw

1. Choose an idea

Start with free drawing or a daily drawing challenge. In free mode, you can give your picture a title.

2. Draw with hidden ink

Use a mouse, finger or pen. Your pointer moves, but the lines stay invisible until the reveal.

3. Reveal your own strokes

Summon the ghost to replay the lines you drew. This is your drawing, not an AI-generated replacement.

4. Keep or share the result

Revealing saves the drawing data in this browser's gallery. Share an image or create a public replay link; friends can open it and try the same prompt.

What is blind drawing?

Draw something without seeing the ink, then let the ghost reveal every stroke. Try a daily prompt or your own idea, and invite a friend to draw the same thing.

Does Ghost Draw use AI to make my drawing?

No. Ghost Draw records your own strokes and plays them back. It does not generate a replacement picture.

Where are my drawings saved?

The website saves drafts and finished drawings in this browser. They are not automatically synced with the iPhone app or another browser. Clearing site data removes them.

Can friends play the same prompt?

Yes. A shared replay can open the same prompt in the web game. Friends can draw their own version without signing in.

What does the iPhone app add?

The app offers a native drawing experience and optional iCloud gallery sync. The browser game can be played independently; installing the app is optional.

Can I play this drawing game online for free?

Yes. The browser game is free and needs no download or account. It includes free drawing, daily prompts, a local gallery and replay sharing.

How can friends play a blind drawing challenge together?

Draw a prompt and share its replay link. Friends can open the same prompt and make their own version. This is an asynchronous same-prompt challenge, not a live multiplayer room.
