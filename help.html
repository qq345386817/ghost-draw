How to use Ghost Draw

1. Pick a challenge

Use the daily prompt or choose a random level. Prompts are intentionally odd so the reveal has personality.

2. Draw blind

The brush cursor follows your finger, but the ink remains hidden. Switch tools, colors, and brush size before or during the attempt.

3. Summon the reveal

Ghost Draw replays your recorded strokes and turns the hidden drawing into a result card.

4. Save or share

Save a result image, keep favorites in Gallery, or share a replay page that loops the drawing process.
