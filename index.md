# Ghost Draw: Free blind drawing game & daily challenges

> Play a free blind drawing game online: hidden ink, daily prompts and surprising stroke reveals. Get the iPhone and iPad app for 2–8 player local Party and replay videos.

Draw something without seeing the ink, then let the ghost reveal every stroke. Try a daily prompt or your own idea, and invite a friend to draw the same thing.

The browser game needs JavaScript. You can still read the guide or get the iPhone and iPad app.

## Help

### 1. Choose an idea

Start with free drawing or a daily drawing challenge. In free mode, you can give your picture a title.

### 2. Draw with hidden ink

Use a mouse, finger or pen. Your pointer moves, but the lines stay invisible until the reveal.

### 3. Reveal your own strokes

Summon the ghost to replay the lines you drew. This is your drawing, not an AI-generated replacement.

### 4. Keep or share the result

Revealing saves the drawing data in this browser's gallery. Share an image or create a public replay link; friends can open it and try the same prompt.

## Ghost Draw for iPhone and iPad

Draw without seeing your lines, then let the ghost reveal your own strokes. The app adds same-device Party, drawing twists and replay videos to turn surprising results into something to laugh about together.

### 2–8 players, one device

Pass the same iPhone or iPad around. Everyone draws the same prompt in timed turns, reveals their result and votes for someone else's drawing. No online opponents or separate devices are needed.

### A new twist on each drawing

Try six optional drawing twists, including one continuous stroke, mirror drawing and upside-down drawing. Pick brushes, shapes and colors, then switch the ghost's commentary style after the reveal. This replays your strokes, not an AI-generated picture.

### Keep the moment, share the surprise

Save and favorite drawings in Gallery, replay them later, or share an image card or silent replay video. Share Party results as a montage and save selected drawings individually; player names, votes and the full Party session are not saved.

Core drawing, replay and local Party work offline with bundled prompts. Catalog updates, Featured and hosted replay links need internet. Creating a link uploads a public copy; community submissions are reviewed before appearing. Optional private iCloud Gallery sync requires iCloud and an Apple Account. There is no separate Ghost Draw account, and browser drawings do not automatically sync with the app.

## Does Ghost Draw use AI to make my drawing?

No. Ghost Draw records your own strokes and plays them back. It does not generate a replacement picture.

## Where are my drawings saved?

The website saves drafts and finished drawings in this browser. They are not automatically synced with the iPhone app or another browser. Clearing site data removes them.

## Can friends play the same prompt?

Yes. A shared replay can open the same prompt in the web game. Friends can draw their own version without signing in.

## What does the iPhone app add?

The app offers a native drawing experience and optional iCloud gallery sync. The browser game can be played independently; installing the app is optional.

## Can I play this drawing game online for free?

Yes. The browser game is free and needs no download or account. It includes free drawing, daily prompts, a local gallery and replay sharing.

## How can friends play a blind drawing challenge together?

Draw a prompt and share its replay link. Friends can open the same prompt and make their own version. This is an asynchronous same-prompt challenge, not a live multiplayer room.

## Is Party mode available in the browser?

Party is an iPhone and iPad app feature for 2–8 players on one device, with timed turns, reveals and voting. In the browser, friends can instead open a shared replay link and draw the same prompt at their own pace; there is no live multiplayer room.

## Can I share the drawing as a video?

The iPhone and iPad app exports a silent MP4 replay of your recorded strokes. The browser game supports image sharing and public replay links, but does not export videos.

## Privacy & statistics

When you share a replay link, drawing strokes, titles, comments and an optional preview image are uploaded. Anyone with the link can view them. Links do not expire automatically; deleting a local or iCloud gallery record does not withdraw a shared link. Contact support for removal requests.

Featured submissions include a separate random creator identifier to remember submission status. A nickname is shown only when you enable public attribution. Likes use another random identifier to prevent duplicate votes. These identifiers are separate from usage-statistics identifiers.
